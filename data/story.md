## Story so far

The 2026 Ebola outbreak began in early May in the Democratic Republic of the Congo's Ituri Province. The earliest known symptom onset is April 24; authorities formally declared the outbreak on May 15. Laboratory analysis at the Institut National de Recherche Biomedicale identified the pathogen as **Bundibugyo virus (BDBV)**, one of four orthoebolavirus species that cause Ebola disease in humans. Unlike the more common Zaire ebolavirus, Bundibugyo currently has no approved vaccine or specific therapeutic.

The World Health Organization declared the outbreak a [Public Health Emergency of International Concern](https://www.who.int/news/item/17-05-2026-epidemic-of-ebola-disease-in-the-democratic-republic-of-the-congo-and-uganda-determined-a-public-health-emergency-of-international-concern) on May 17. Africa CDC declared a Public Health Emergency of Continental Security on May 18. Within the first ten days, cases spread across 12 health zones in three DRC provinces (Ituri, Nord-Kivu, Sud-Kivu) plus imported and locally-transmitted cases in Kampala, Uganda.

The response is operating under acute constraints: armed conflict in the affected zones, community distrust of containment measures (multiple Ebola treatment centers have been attacked, with patients escaping), high population mobility, and the absence of a Bundibugyo-specific vaccine. Healthcare-associated infections in Uganda raise concerns about undetected transmission chains in urban centers.

## Latest update
**August 9.** DRC stands at **4,294 confirmed cases, 1,960 deaths and 849 recovered**. With Uganda's final 20 and the single case in France that is **4,315 confirmed and 1,962 deaths** globally. The figure comes from the health ministry's own [situation report](https://x.com/Com_mediasRDC), and this time two wires carry it independently, [EFE](https://www.infobae.com/america/agencias/2026/08/10/la-rd-del-congo-aumenta-a-1960-la-cifra-de-muertos-y-4294-los-casos-confirmados-de-ebola/) and Europa Press. Both date the data to August 8. August 10 and 11 stay gaps. BNO News has not published a daily graphic since August 8, the longest gap since mid-July, so there is no second daily instrument to fall back on right now.

The previous entry flagged a single good day and said it was a data point, not a turn. It was not a turn. Deaths rose by 44 against the 29 that produced that dip, and the case-fatality rate went back to climbing, 45.5 to 45.6 percent in DRC. Active cases, which are confirmed minus recovered minus deaths, went from 1,465 to 1,485. That is what a running wire-grade count does. One quiet day inside a series growing this fast is usually the reporting, not the epidemic.

Two things from that day did carry through. Contact tracing reached **84.6 percent**, up from 83.4 and from 75 percent when the WHO and Africa CDC mission measured it on August 4 and 5. Three bulletins in a row moving up, now within half a point of the 85 percent operational threshold. And the occupancy number that did not fit last time has resolved itself. Isolation beds went 721, then 595, then 722. A 126-patient drop that reverses completely the next bulletin is a reporting artefact, not beds lost. Worth saying plainly, because the previous entry left it open.

The outbreak has now passed the 2018 to 2020 Nord-Kivu epidemic, which recorded 3,481 cases and 2,299 deaths. By case count this is the second largest Ebola outbreak ever recorded, behind only West Africa in 2014 to 2016. It got there in under three months. West Africa took about two years to reach 28,000.

The [European CDC](https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en/ebola-outbreak-democratic-republic-congo-and-uganda) published the provincial split for August 7, and it is the clearest picture of where this is concentrated. Ituri holds 3,636 cases and 1,551 deaths across 28 of its 36 health zones. Nord-Kivu holds 470 cases and 315 deaths. Haut-Uele 91, Tshopo 9, Sud-Kivu 3. So Ituri is 86 percent of the cases and 81 percent of the deaths, and Sud-Kivu, which has gone 73 days without a new confirmed case, is effectively finished. This is one province with a satellite, not five provinces burning.

The IHR Emergency Committee meets on August 18.
